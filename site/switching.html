Switching to Centaur

Moving from another CRM? We'll guide you through it.

During the beta, we join you on a Microsoft Teams call and guide your import step by step, at no cost. Your data stays in your hands: you run the import into your own workspace, and we make sure every field lands in the right place.

How it works

  1. We book a Teams call. At a time that suits you, with whoever looks after your CRM data today.
  2. You export your data. Every major CRM can export to a spreadsheet: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Dynamics 365, monday CRM, Zoho and the rest. We send you a short guide for yours. Spreadsheets you keep yourself work too, in Excel or CSV. The files stay on your computer.
  3. We set up Centaur to match, together. On the call, we help you set up your pipeline stages to match the ones you use now, and add your team, so each record goes to the right owner.
  4. You import, with us alongside. You share your screen and run the import under Admin → Data Imports. We help you match each column to the right field, and anything Centaur doesn't have becomes a custom field, so nothing is lost.
  5. Check a sample, then load the rest. Import a handful of records first and check them in Centaur together. Once they look right, bring in the rest: companies, contacts, deals, leads and activity history, linked together.
  6. Run side by side, if you like. Keep your old CRM for a few weeks while your team settles in. Centaur never connects to it, so nothing there changes.

What comes across

Imported

  • Companies (accounts)
  • Contacts, linked to their company
  • Deals, with stage, value, close date and owner
  • Leads
  • Activity history: calls, meetings, notes and tasks
  • Your own fields, as custom fields

Set up fresh in Centaur

  • Reports and dashboards
  • Workflows and automations
  • Email templates and sequences

Your email doesn't need moving: once Microsoft 365 is connected, the agents work from your inbox and calendar from day one.

Prefer to do it on your own?

You can. The call is there if you want it, not a requirement. Under Admin → Data Imports, Centaur suggests a match for each column, shows sample values so you can check them, and creates custom fields for anything that doesn't fit. We're on hand if you get stuck.

Your data during the move

  • Your files never leave your hands. You run the import from your own computer, straight into your own workspace, and we don't receive or keep a copy.
  • On the call, we only see what you choose to share on screen.
  • We never ask for a login to your old CRM.
  • Everything goes into your own workspace, protected as described on our Security page.
