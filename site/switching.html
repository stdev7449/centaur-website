Switching to Centaur

Moving from another CRM? We'll move your data for you.

During the beta, we move your companies, contacts, deals and history into Centaur for you, at no cost. You send us the exports, we do the matching and the import, and you check the result before your team starts.

How it works

  1. You export your data. Every major CRM can export to a spreadsheet: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Dynamics 365, monday CRM, Zoho and the rest. We send you a short guide for yours. Spreadsheets you keep yourself work too, in Excel or CSV.
  2. We match it to Centaur. Each of your columns goes to the right field. Anything Centaur doesn't have becomes a custom field, so nothing is lost. We set up your pipeline stages to match the ones you use now, and match each record's owner to the right person on your team.
  3. You check a sample. Before we load everything, you see how a handful of your records look in Centaur, and tell us what to change.
  4. We import it. Companies, contacts, deals, leads and activity history come across linked together: each contact to their company, each deal to its account and contact.
  5. You run side by side, if you like. Keep your old CRM for a few weeks while your team settles in. Centaur never connects to it, so nothing there changes.

What comes across

Moved for you

  • Companies (accounts)
  • Contacts, linked to their company
  • Deals, with stage, value, close date and owner
  • Leads
  • Activity history: calls, meetings, notes and tasks
  • Your own fields, as custom fields

Set up fresh in Centaur

  • Reports and dashboards
  • Workflows and automations
  • Email templates and sequences

Your email doesn't need moving: once Microsoft 365 is connected, the agents work from your inbox and calendar from day one.

Prefer to do it yourself?

You can. Your Centaur admin can import spreadsheets under Admin → Data Imports. Centaur suggests a match for each column, shows sample values so you can check them, and creates custom fields for anything that doesn't fit. We're on hand if you get stuck.

Your data during the move

  • We only work from the exports you send us. We never ask for a login to your old CRM.
  • Your files are used for your import only, and we delete them once you've confirmed it's right.
  • Everything goes into your own workspace, protected as described on our Security page.

Ready to switch?

Tell us which CRM you use now and roughly how many records you have, and we'll plan your move with you.
