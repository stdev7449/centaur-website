Security

Your data, looked after.

We're a young company, so we don't hold our own ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certificates yet. They're on the roadmap. What we've done instead is build Centaur on providers that do, and lock down everything that's ours to control.

What we look after ourselves

  • Every connection is encrypted, and browsers are told never to connect without it.
  • Passwords are hashed (scrypt). We never see or store them.
  • Your keys for AI, Microsoft 365 and your data provider are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they're stored.
  • Each company's data lives in its own workspace, checked on the server on every request.
  • Role-based access: you decide what each role can see and do.
  • Sign-in attempts are rate limited to stop password guessing.
  • We review our code for security regularly and fix what we find.

Backups and recovery

  • Every workspace is backed up every night, as one file encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it leaves our servers.
  • We keep every nightly backup for 30 days, then one a month for a year, so we can restore a workspace if something goes badly wrong.
  • Deleted records go to a recycle bin for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone without us.

Control and accountability

  • An audit log records every change to every record: who changed what, and when. Only our servers can write to it.
  • Admins choose how long someone can be idle before they're signed out automatically.
  • Everything the AI agents do is recorded in AI Activity, with what they sent and who approved it.

If you leave

  • Your company has 30 days to take a full export of its data.
  • We then delete your workspace. Encrypted backups that contain it are removed as they age out, within 12 months.
  • Your AI provider and Microsoft 365 connections are yours: revoke them at any time from your own accounts.

The certifications above belong to our providers. Centaur CRM itself is not yet certified. We're happy to share the full list of providers, with their certificates, with every company that joins the beta. Found a security issue? Email info@centaurcrm.com and we'll get straight on it.
