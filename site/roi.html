Where the time comes from

Each line in the calculator is work one of the agents takes on.

Email replies and meetings bookedThe Opener answers inquiries in your rep's voice and books the call from their calendar.
Deal updatesThe Analyst reads every email and call note and keeps the deal up to date.
Call notesThe Scribe writes up recorded Teams calls and files them on the deal.
Meeting briefsThe Briefer prepares a brief for every call on the calendar.

The figure is time saved alone. It doesn't count the extra deals your reps can work on with that time back.
