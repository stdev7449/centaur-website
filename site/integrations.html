Integrations

Works with the tools your team already uses.

Centaur is built around Microsoft 365 and your own AI account, with notifications, data providers and your website joined up. Here’s what connects today, and what’s coming next.

Available now

Integrations marked Beta are built and working in the app, and we’re still testing them with more customer setups.

Email & calendarMicrosoft 365: Outlook email

The agents read and reply to email in your reps’ own mailboxes and the shared inbox, and keep deals up to date from the conversation.

Setup: Your IT admin registers one app in Microsoft (about 20 minutes, with a step-by-step guide); each rep then connects their mailbox.

Email & calendarMicrosoft 365: Outlook calendar

Offers real free times, books meetings and sends the invite, with a Teams link if that’s how you meet.

Setup: Included with the Microsoft 365 connection.

CallsMicrosoft Teams call transcripts

Recorded Teams calls become a written summary with next steps, and blank fields on the deal are filled in.

Setup: One Teams admin setting allows Centaur to read transcripts.

AIYour own Anthropic account

Runs every AI agent on your own account, billed by Anthropic at their rate with no markup from us.

Setup: Paste your API key in Admin → AI Connector.

NotificationsSlack Beta

Alerts, one-tap approvals for drafts and a daily digest, in direct messages.

Setup: Add the Centaur app to your Slack workspace.

NotificationsMicrosoft Teams channel posts Beta

Posts AI activity and alerts to a Teams channel.

Setup: Add an incoming webhook to the channel.

ProspectingApollo Beta

Find new prospects and enrich leads and contacts from your Apollo account.

Setup: Add your Apollo API key in Admin → Data Providers.

ProspectingPeople Data Labs, Lusha and ZoomInfo Beta

Enrich records and find new prospects from the data provider you already use.

Setup: Add your provider’s API key in Admin → Data Providers.

Your dataSpreadsheet import (Excel and CSV)

Bring in accounts, contacts, leads, deals and activities, with columns matched for you and custom fields for anything that doesn’t fit. During the beta, we’ll guide your import on a Teams call.

Setup: Admin → Data Imports.

Your websiteForms, landing pages and gated downloads

Website forms and landing pages create leads in Centaur; offer a document and it is emailed to the person straight away, with the agents ready for their reply.

Setup: Build pages in Centaur, or post your own website form to Centaur.
