Automated CRM data entry

A CRM that updates itself from your emails and calls.

Reps lose hours a week typing up what a buyer already said in an email or on a call, and when they're busy, the CRM falls behind. Centaur reads the conversation and keeps the deal up to date for them, without ever writing over what a person entered.

What gets filled in automatically

New deals, created for you

When an inquiry turns into a booked meeting, the account, contact and opportunity are created, with everything learned in the conversation already on them.

Qualification

Each field of your sales methodology, such as BANT or MEDDPIC, filled in from what the buyer said.

Value, quantity and close date

Set from the conversation, for example 40 users at your list price, with the reasoning shown beside the figure.

Competitors

Rivals named in an email are added to the deal as the incumbent or a challenger, and linked to your battle cards.

Risks

A slipping timeline, a new competitor or a budget worry is flagged on the deal with the exact quote, and marked as addressed when the buyer settles it.

Deal summary and call notes

A running summary of the deal, plus a write-up of each recorded Teams call: what was discussed, what was agreed and the next steps.

Where it comes from

  • Email: the conversation with the buyer, in your reps' Microsoft 365 mailboxes.
  • Calls: recorded Microsoft Teams calls, and the notes reps log after any other call.
  • Your CRM: your own sales methodology, products, prices and battle cards, so updates are in your terms.

The rules it follows

  • It fills blank fields only. A value a person typed in is never overwritten.
  • Where it would change a value, such as the deal amount or close date, it suggests the new one, and a rep applies it with one click.
  • Every change is listed on the deal, with where it came from.
  • It doesn't move deals between stages on its own. That stays with your reps, or with your own workflow rules.
  • It never sends anything to the buyer. Replies are drafted by the outreach agent and approved by a person, unless you choose otherwise.

An example

Before the first call, the buyer emails again: they now need 55 users instead of 40, they want to sign by December 15, and they're also looking at HubSpot. Soon after the email arrives, the deal shows a suggested new value and close date, HubSpot as a challenger, a competitor risk with the buyer's own words, and a refreshed summary. The rep clicks Apply twice, and the deal is current. See it in the demo video.

Questions

Will it overwrite what my reps have entered?

No. The agents fill in blank fields only. Where they'd change a value a person entered, such as the deal value or close date, they suggest the new one and a rep applies it with one click.

Where does the information come from?

From the email conversation with the buyer, the notes reps log after calls, and recorded Microsoft Teams calls. Risks are flagged with the exact quote, so you can see where each one came from.

Does it change the deal stage?

Not on its own. Stage changes are yours, or your own workflow rules, for example moving a deal on once a demo is marked complete.

What do I need?

Microsoft 365 for email, calendar and Teams, and your own Anthropic account for the AI. Teams call summaries need one Teams admin setting switched on.
