Workflow automation for the routine stuff

Pick a trigger, add some conditions and choose what happens, with waits and multi-step sequences if you need them. When an activity is completed, tell the owner. When a deal stalls, give someone a nudge. New rules start switched off, so you can test them on your real data before turning them on.

AI in the fabric, not on top

Every other CRM added AI. Centaur is made of it.

AI will never replace people: it can't match the empathy a real person brings to a customer relationship. But it can take on the work around them. Centaur was born in the age of AI and built around it, not bolted onto a product designed years before.

Context is everything. You train Centaur like a new rep: your business, products, competitors, sales process and how you qualify deals. Then it just works, and the best way to see it is to try it.

AI bolted on

the way it's usually done
  • An extra you pay for, on a separate line of the invoice
  • It suggests some text, but a person still has to do the work
  • Rationed with AI credits, so the more you use it, the more you pay
  • Surface-level: a few writing tips and a basic forecast
  • Added to a system that was designed years before AI existed

AI in the fabric

the way Centaur works
  • Built in, with no AI credits to count and no add-on to buy
  • Agents that read your emails and act on them: they reply, qualify, book meetings and update the CRM
  • You pay your AI provider directly, at their rate, with no AI upsell from us
  • It goes deep: deal risk, next best action and records that keep themselves up to date
  • Built around the agents from the very start, not the other way around

Everything else it does

Everything else you'd want from a CRM, without the busywork.

Pipeline & forecasting

Set up your own stages and sales methodology. Price products per user, per site or as a one-off item, sell in more than one currency at your own exchange rates, and let forecast automation keep each deal's rating and category up to date.

Campaigns & landing pages

Build emails with a drag-and-drop editor, send them from your own mailbox, publish landing pages, run A/B tests and see which campaigns turned into won revenue.

Prospecting & lead routing

Search your connected data provider, such as Apollo, for people who match your ideal customer and add them in one click. Then hand out leads to reps in rotation or by your own rules before any agent gets involved, with a holding queue for your sales director when a lead needs a human decision.

Roles & permissions

Detailed permissions, your own roles, easy record reassignment, a 30-day recycle bin for anything deleted and a full audit trail that shows who (or which agent) changed what.

Built-in training

Self-paced learning guides inside the platform, so new reps can get up to speed without pulling anyone else away from selling.

Slack & Teams

Approve a draft, respond to an escalation or add a lead straight from a direct message. A daily digest and a home tab keep each rep in the loop, in the tools they already use.

Integrations

Works with the tools your team already uses.

Centaur is built around Microsoft 365: the agents work in your reps' Outlook mailboxes and calendars, and turn recorded Teams calls into notes. The AI runs on your own Anthropic account. Alerts and approvals go to Slack or Teams, prospects come from the data provider you already use, and your website forms and landing pages feed straight in. A public API, Zapier, single sign-on with Entra ID or Okta, Google Workspace and Zoom are on the roadmap.

Getting started

Up and running the same day. No consultants needed.

As soon as you sign up, you're in, and a guided setup walks you through everything. Give it your website, and the AI drafts a brief about your business for you to check. Upload the spreadsheets you already have, and Centaur matches the columns for you. Your IT admin gets the email setup steps in one click.

  • Send your IT admin the Microsoft 365 steps and invite your reps from a list
  • Enter your website, and the AI drafts your product, market and positioning for you to correct
  • Import your spreadsheets as they are, with custom fields for any columns that don't fit. Moving from another CRM? We'll guide you through it.
  • Choose whether the agents draft replies for you to check or run on their own
  • Sit back and watch the first inquiries come in!
