Centaur CRM vs Dynamics 365 Sales. Which suits your team?

Dynamics 365 Sales is Microsoft's own CRM, and for many organizations it is the right choice. Centaur also runs on Microsoft 365, so this is the comparison most of our prospects ask about. Here is what Dynamics does well, how its AI is charged, and where Centaur is different, so you can decide.

What Dynamics 365 Sales is known for

Deep ties to the rest of Microsoft: Outlook and Teams, Power BI reporting, Power Platform for custom apps and automation, and Dynamics finance and operations systems. It can be shaped to almost any sales process, which is why large organizations with complex needs choose it.

That flexibility usually comes with a project. Most organizations set it up with a Microsoft partner, and someone needs to look after it once it is live.

How Dynamics 365's AI is charged

Copilot is included in the higher Dynamics 365 Sales plans. The sales agents, built in Copilot Studio, run on Copilot Credits. Only the top plan comes with a monthly allowance of credits; beyond that, credits are bought in capacity packs or paid for as they are used.

With Centaur, you connect your own AI account and pay your AI provider directly, at their rate. There are no credits to buy, no per-action fees and no markup.

Where Centaur differs

Centaur works with the Microsoft 365 you already have: the agents reply from your reps' Outlook mailboxes, book from their calendars and summarize recorded Teams calls. The difference is what comes ready to use. Centaur's agents answer and qualify inbound inquiries, book the meeting and keep every deal current from day one, without a Copilot Studio project, a partner or credits to manage.

Dynamics goes further where you need it to: custom data models, ERP, field service and the wider Power Platform. If you need those, Dynamics is the stronger choice.

Choose Dynamics 365 Sales if

You are a large organization standardized on Microsoft, you need a CRM shaped around complex processes or tied into Dynamics ERP, and you have a partner or in-house team to run it.

Choose Centaur if

You are a B2B sales team of roughly 15 to 200 on Microsoft 365, inbound inquiries wait too long for a reply, and you want AI agents that work from the first day, with no implementation project and no AI markup.
