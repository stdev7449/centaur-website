Our story

Thirty years selling. One recurring frustration.

Centaur was founded and built by a sales professional with 30 years of selling experience — not by a software developer.

Imagine if you had the opportunity to build a CRM that did exactly what you wanted to make your job easier: something that helps you manage deals from the first inquiry right through to closing, where all you have to do is show up to the meetings and the CRM does the rest, automatically.

Imagine if your CRM were completely up to date, all the time, so that when you have your pipeline review with your manager, every deal is exactly where it needs to be: close date, pipeline stage, deal value, qualification and forecast status. Imagine walking into that meeting confident of the number you're carrying.

Well, this is what I imagined… and now it's a reality!

For too long, I was torn between spending more time selling and making time to update the CRM. The busier we get as sales professionals, the less time we have for admin, writing follow-up emails or even trying to generate new leads. There are only so many hours in the day, and I wanted to spend as many of them as I could talking to customers and moving deals forward, not wasting time on admin!

If this sounds familiar, I built Centaur for you!

You see, I know CRM tools. I've implemented a couple in my years as a sales guy, and let me tell you, I had a huge list of things I wanted to be able to do to make my team's lives easier, but there were always too many blockers. Either the solution couldn't do it, or we'd have to buy another tool and integrate it, or it would cost a fortune to bring in a consultant to build it. So I decided enough was enough and built my own.

The problem was, it turned out better than I ever expected. It was too good to keep to myself, so now I'm offering it to you.

If you could give every rep an assistant for the price of a cup of coffee every day — one that never logs off at five, never calls in sick, never takes a vacation — would you?
