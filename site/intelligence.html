Intelligence and reporting

See your whole pipeline, without asking for a spreadsheet.

Your reps spend their day in deals. Centaur gives you the whole picture: the forecast, how each rep is doing, what buyers are doing right now and what's changing across the pipeline. Every number is counted from your own records. Nothing is estimated, and nothing is made up.

Pipeline and forecast

How much is in the pipeline, what will close, and what's in trouble.

The Pipeline summary answers all three on one screen, for your own deals, your team's or the whole company's. Open pipeline, new deals this week, win rate and days to close, each with its trend over the last eight weeks. Pipeline by stage, and the quarter's forecast (won, commit and best case) against your target.

Then the list that matters most: deals needing attention. A close date that's passed, no activity in two weeks, or a deal closing in the next few days, most urgent first. With forecast automation on, each deal's rating and forecast status follow what's actually happening on it.

Team performance

Know who's on track, and who needs help, before the quarter ends.

The Team dashboard puts every rep's numbers side by side: deals won and lost, open pipeline and how well their deals are qualified. Click any number to see the deals behind it.

The Team briefing sums it up in plain words, then shows pipeline velocity (new deals this week against the four-week average, for the team and each rep), a target tracker with where each rep should be by now, stalled deals, overdue tasks and competitor contracts coming up for renewal.

Dashboards and reports

Your numbers, your way, always up to date.

Anyone can build their own dashboard in My Dashboards: pipeline value, win rate, average deal size, the sales funnel, quota attainment, a rep leaderboard, an activity heatmap and more. If the chart you want isn't there, build it in a few clicks: pick the records, filter, group, and choose a bar, line, pie or funnel chart.

The Reports screen covers the standard questions: pipeline by stage, team KPIs, revenue by product, the forecast and campaign results. Every chart reads the records your team works in, so nobody exports anything.

Signals and Intelligence

Know what's changing, before it shows in the numbers.

Signals is a feed of things that have actually happened and are worth acting on: a contact clicked a link, someone replied after months of silence, a competitor's contract is up for renewal, a deal has stalled or the Analyst flagged a risk. Open the record, create a task for the rep, or mark it done.

Intelligence looks across every conversation and deal each week, and tells you what keeps coming up: the competitors turning up, the objections coming back, why deals are lost, where they get stuck and what your wins have in common. Each finding shows how sure it is, its trend and the evidence behind it.

Counted, not guessed

Every figure on these screens comes from the deals, activities and emails already in Centaur. Where the AI helps, as it does in Intelligence, it explains a finding in plain words. It can't add a finding or change a number.
