CRM for Microsoft 365

A CRM that works inside Microsoft 365.

Your reps already spend the day in Outlook, their calendar and Teams. Centaur works with all three, so the CRM records what happens there by itself, and the AI agents reply, book meetings and write up calls from the same place.

What it does with each part of Microsoft 365

Outlook email

Emails with customers and prospects are recorded on the right account, contact and deal. The AI agents read the conversation and reply from the rep's own mailbox, as drafts for approval or on their own.

The shared inbox

Inquiries sent to a shared address, such as info@, are picked up for a rep to add as a lead in one click, and the outreach agent drafts the reply.

Outlook calendar

The agents offer real free times from the rep's calendar, book the meeting and send the invite, with a Teams link if that's how you meet.

Microsoft Teams calls

Recorded Teams calls become a written summary of what was discussed and agreed, with next steps, and blank fields on the deal are filled in.

Teams channels

AI activity and alerts can be posted to a Teams channel, so the team sees what the agents did.

Email campaigns

Campaigns are sent from your own Microsoft 365 mailboxes, so they come from your own domain.

Setting it up

  • Your IT admin registers one app in Microsoft, with a step-by-step guide. It takes about 20 minutes.
  • Each rep then connects their own mailbox and calendar.
  • Teams call summaries need one Teams admin setting switched on.

The guided setup sends your IT admin the steps, so sales doesn't have to explain them.

What it doesn't do yet

Centaur works with Microsoft 365 only for now. Gmail and Google Calendar are on the roadmap, as are an Outlook add-in and a Teams app.

Questions

Does Centaur work with Outlook?

Yes. Centaur works with Outlook email and the Outlook calendar in Microsoft 365. Emails and meetings are recorded on the right record, and the AI agents reply and book meetings from the rep's own mailbox and calendar.

Does Centaur work with Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Meetings can be booked with a Teams link, recorded Teams calls are written up as call notes on the deal, and AI activity can be posted to a Teams channel.

Do the AI agents send email from our own mailboxes?

Yes. Replies go from the rep's own Microsoft 365 mailbox, in their voice. By default each reply is a draft that a person approves; you choose which reps the agent may send for on its own.

What does our IT team need to do?

Register one app in Microsoft, which takes about 20 minutes with our step-by-step guide. For Teams call summaries, they also switch on one Teams admin setting.

Does Centaur work with Google Workspace?

Not yet. Centaur works with Microsoft 365 only for now. Gmail and Google Calendar are on our roadmap.
