Meet the agents

Six AI agents doing the work your reps would otherwise do by hand.

More conversations, without hiring more people. The agents take care of the outreach, the CRM updates and the meeting prep, so the team you already have can handle the pipeline you'd otherwise have to hire for.

The Opener · Outreach agent

Answers the inquiry, books the meeting and opens the deal.

When an email comes in, the agent replies in your rep's voice and qualifies the prospect against your sales methodology as the conversation goes. It offers real free times from the rep's calendar, and once a time is confirmed, it sends the invite and creates the account, contact and opportunity, with everything it learned carried onto the new deal. You decide whether it drafts replies for you to check or runs on its own.

"Thursday at 10 works — I've sent the invite. Ahead of the call, roughly how many seats would you be looking at?"

The Analyst · Opportunity agent

No more deals going dark on you.

Once a deal is open, your rep owns the conversation, but Centaur keeps reading the emails and the notes your reps log after each call. If a budget is mentioned, a new stakeholder appears, the timeline slips or a competitor's name comes up, it updates the record, keeps a running summary of the deal and flags the risk with the exact quote. It never sends anything itself.

So the answer the buyer gave you last Tuesday is on the opportunity, not lost in someone's inbox.

The Briefer · Meeting brief agent

Walk into every call fully prepared.

Every morning, for each call on the calendar, the agent pulls together the deal's history: how long it's been open, its stage, recent activity and any competitors or risks already flagged. It also researches the company on the web. The brief lands as a note on the deal, on the rep's Today page and in their Slack messages.

Ten minutes before the call, your rep knows the deal inside out, plus something about the company that was never in the CRM.

Watch the Briefer prepare a call1:33
The Scribe · Teams call agent

Your calls, written up and filed.

The Scribe listens to your recorded Microsoft Teams calls and writes them up: what was discussed, what was agreed and the next steps. The summary goes on the deal, and anything new the buyer said, like a budget, a date or a new person involved, is added to the record. Nobody types up notes after the call.

By the time your rep has closed Teams, the call is on the deal.

The Copywriter · Sequences

Personal emails, at the scale of a campaign.

The Copywriter writes a personal version of every email in your outreach sequences, using each person's role, company and industry. A rep approves every one before it goes, and if someone replies, their sequence stops straight away, so nobody gets a scripted follow-up after they've answered.

Forty personal emails, without writing forty emails.

The Co-pilot · AI assistant

Ask your pipeline anything.

The Co-pilot answers questions about your pipeline in plain English, from the records in your CRM. It can log calls and tasks and update deals too, but only after you click Apply, so nothing changes without you seeing it first.

"Which deals are closing this month?" Answered in seconds, with the deals to back it up.
