AI sales agents

AI sales agents that do the work, not just suggest it.

Most CRMs now add an AI assistant that suggests text for a rep to use. Centaur's agents do the work itself: they answer inquiries, qualify and book meetings, keep every deal up to date from email and calls, and brief your reps before each call. You decide how much they do on their own.

The six agents

Outreach agentThe Opener

Answers each inbound inquiry in your rep's voice, qualifies the prospect against your sales methodology as the conversation goes, offers real free times from the rep's calendar and books the meeting. Then it creates the account, contact and opportunity, with everything it learned already on the deal.

Opportunity agentThe Analyst

Once a deal is open, it reads the emails and the notes reps log after calls. When a budget, a new stakeholder, a slipping timeline or a competitor comes up, it updates the deal, keeps a running summary and flags the risk with the exact quote. It never sends anything, and never overwrites a figure a person typed in.

Meeting brief agentThe Briefer

Every morning, for each call on the calendar, it writes a brief: the deal's history, its stage, recent activity, the risks already flagged, and web research on the company. It lands on the deal, on the rep's Today page and in Slack.

Call notesThe Scribe

Turns recorded Microsoft Teams calls into a summary of what was discussed, what was agreed and the next steps, and fills in blank fields on the deal.

SequencesThe Copywriter

Writes a personal version of every email in an outreach sequence, using each person's role, company and industry. A rep approves every one before it goes.

AssistantThe Co-pilot

Answers questions about your pipeline in plain English, logs calls and tasks and updates deals, but only after you click Apply.

How they work together

  1. An inquiry arrives by email, from a campaign reply, your website or a forwarded introduction.
  2. The outreach agent replies, handles objections using your battle cards and qualifies the prospect against your methodology, such as BANT or MEDDPIC.
  3. It offers real free times, confirms one and sends the invite, with a Teams link if that's how you meet.
  4. The account, contact and opportunity are created, with what the agent learned already on the record.
  5. From then on, the opportunity agent reads every reply and call note and keeps the deal current, while the brief agent prepares the rep for each call.

You stay in control

  • Outreach drafts wait for a person to approve them, until you choose to let an agent send on its own.
  • One switch pauses every agent.
  • The agents never overwrite a value a person typed in; where they'd change one, they suggest it instead.
  • Deal updates come only from your emails, calls and notes, and risks are flagged with the exact quote, so you can check where each one came from.

The AI is yours, at cost

You connect your own Anthropic account and pay Anthropic directly, at their rate. There are no AI credits to buy and no markup from us, so using the agents more doesn't push up your CRM bill.

Questions

What is an AI sales agent?

Software that does a piece of sales work on its own, not just suggest text. In Centaur, the agents read your email and calendar, reply to inquiries, book meetings and update deal records, within rules you set.

Will the agents email my prospects without me knowing?

Not unless you choose that. By default every outreach draft waits for a person to approve it. Once you trust an agent you can let it send on its own, and one switch pauses all of them.

Which AI does Centaur use, and what does it cost?

You connect your own Anthropic account. You pay Anthropic directly, at their rate, with no credits to buy and no markup from us.

What do I need to run them?

Microsoft 365 for email and calendar, and your own Anthropic account. Google Workspace isn't supported yet.
