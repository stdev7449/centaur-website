Campaigns and tracking

From first campaign click to closed deal.

Centaur runs your email campaigns inside the CRM, so it can follow each buyer from the email to the meeting and the deal. You see who clicked, which link, who replied, and the meetings, deals and pipeline that followed each campaign, without exporting a single list.

One buyer's journey

What happens after you press Send.

1

The campaign goes out

Build it from blocks or a saved template, test two subject lines, and send it from your own Microsoft 365 mailbox to a marketing list that keeps itself up to date. Centaur sends within the hours and time zone you choose, and skips anyone who has unsubscribed, bounced or asked not to be contacted.

Block composerA/B testsDynamic listsSending hours
2

A buyer clicks a link

Centaur records who clicked and which link. Security scanners that click every link in an email are filtered out, so the click rate is people who clicked out of emails delivered. Links to your own website are tagged, so the visit shows up in your web analytics too.

Clicks per linkScanners filteredUTM tags
3

They land on your page and fill in the form

Landing pages are built in Centaur too. A form filled in becomes a lead with the campaign attached, goes to the right rep, and the Opener agent can send the first reply straight away.

Landing pagesLead routingFirst reply in minutes
4

They reply

A reply to the campaign is counted against it, so you see the reply rate as well as the clicks. Out-of-office replies don't count.

5

A meeting is booked and a deal opened

When someone who clicked or replied has a meeting booked or a deal opened within 30 days, it appears on that campaign's results, with the pipeline value and what was won. A deal that came from the campaign's landing page always counts.

Honest numbers

Email opens are shown as approximate. Apple Mail and many security tools open every email themselves, so an "open" often isn't a person. That's why Centaur leads with clicks and replies, which are.

A meeting or deal is shown as having followed a campaign, not as caused by it. The rule is on the screen: people who clicked or replied, and within 30 days had a meeting booked or a deal opened.

Ask the Co-pilot "which campaign brought in the most pipeline this quarter?" and it answers from the same figures.

One system from campaign to closed deal

Because campaigns, landing pages, leads and deals live in the same CRM, nothing has to be matched up between a marketing tool and a sales tool. The agents see the same history your reps do.
