How AI is charged

AI in your CRM, at cost. No credits, no markup.

Most CRMs charge for AI on top of the seat price: an add-on per user, a monthly allowance of credits, or a fee per action. In Centaur, the AI agents run on your own Anthropic account, and Anthropic bills you directly at their rate. We don't resell it, mark it up or ration it.

The common ways CRMs charge for AI

  • A per-user AI add-on: a fixed extra amount per seat, whether or not each person uses it.
  • Credits: an allowance each month. Usage is hard to predict, and when the credits run out the AI stops or you buy more.
  • A fee per action: a charge for each conversation, summary or record the AI works on.

Each can work, but each makes AI costs hard to see and hard to budget for. Our white paper The true cost of CRM AI goes through them in detail.

How Centaur does it

Your own AI account

You connect your own Anthropic account in Admin → AI Connector. Every AI agent runs on it.

Billed at cost

Anthropic bills you directly, at their published rates. Centaur adds nothing on top.

No credits

There's no allowance to run out at the end of the month. The agents keep working.

Your terms

Your data is processed under your own agreement with Anthropic, so you can read their terms on data use for yourself.

You choose the model

Choose which AI model the agents use, from Anthropic's current models.

You set the limit

Spending limits and usage reports are in your Anthropic account, so you always know what the AI costs.

What it usually costs

We expect AI usage to average $5–$15 per user, per month. It depends on how much the agents do: a team with lots of inbound inquiries and recorded calls uses more than a team without.

Questions

How is the AI charged in Centaur?

The AI agents run on your own Anthropic account, and Anthropic bills you directly at their rate. Centaur does not resell the AI, mark it up or ration it with credits.

How much does the AI cost?

We expect it to average $5–$15 per user, per month, depending on how much the agents do. You can see the exact figure and set a spending limit in your Anthropic account.

What happens if we reach our spending limit?

The AI agents stop until the limit resets or you raise it. The rest of the CRM keeps working as normal.

Do I need a technical person to set it up?

No. You create an Anthropic account, copy your API key and paste it into Admin → AI Connector. We walk you through it during setup.

Is the AI included in the Centaur price?

No, on purpose. The Centaur price covers the CRM and the agents; the AI usage is billed by Anthropic at cost, so you only pay for what you use and can see every cent of it.
