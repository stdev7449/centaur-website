An honest fit check

Centaur is a strong fit when

  • You are a B2B sales team of roughly 15 to 200 people.
  • Your team lives in its inbox and calendar.
  • Inbound inquiries wait too long for a reply, or get missed.
  • Reps spend hours a week updating the CRM instead of selling.
  • You want AI you can switch on in draft mode and trust step by step.

Another CRM may suit you better when

  • You need heavy customization, custom objects or a large app marketplace.
  • Marketing automation matters more to you than sales execution.
  • You are a team of two or three who only need a pipeline board.
  • Your email runs on Google Workspace: Centaur works with Microsoft 365 only, for now.

Five questions that cut through the AI claims

Worth asking any CRM vendor, including us.

Does it act, or only suggest?

Ask to see the AI reply to a real inquiry and book a real meeting, end to end.

What does the AI cost at volume?

Ask for the bill at ten times today's usage, including credits, add-ons and overage.

Who stays in control?

Ask how a draft is approved, what the AI may never promise, and how to pause it.

How long until we are live?

Ask who does the setup, how long it takes and what it costs before the first rep logs in.

Will it overwrite my team's data?

Ask what happens when the AI and a rep disagree about a deal's value or close date.
